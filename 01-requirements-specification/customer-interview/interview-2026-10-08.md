## 3. Working Time and Break Rules

### 3.1 Flexible Working Hours

- Employees may start and finish work at flexible times, depending on customer and project requirements.
- Employees must have sufficient rest time between working days.

### 3.2 Breaks

The customer specified the following break rules:

| Working time | Required break |
|---|---|
| Less than 6 hours | Optional |
| More than 6 hours | 30 minutes |
| More than 9 hours | 60 minutes |

**Open point:** Clarify the rules for exactly 6 hours and exactly 9 hours of work.

## 4. Additional Information from Other Groups

The following information was collected from other groups' questions and answers during the customer interview session.

### 4.1 User Roles and Access

- The system should have an administrator role.
- HR employees are also employees.
- HR should only access the system from company premises.
- Employees and supervisors should be able to access the system from anywhere.
- The customer did not specify a preferred authentication method.

### 4.2 Vacation Management

- Employees can carry over up to 7 vacation days into the following year.
- If carried-over vacation days remain unused, the employee should be notified to use them during the first three months of the new year. Otherwise, the days are lost.
- Employees should inform the company about planned vacation at least one day beforehand.
- When an employee cancels vacation, the supervisor should be notified two days beforehand.
- Employees cannot directly add vacation days to their balance; vacation-time adjustments must be requested through HR.

### 4.3 Sickness

- Sick leave does not require approval.
- The supervisor should take action if an employee's sickness lasts too long.
- The process for recording a supervisor's own sickness or vacation remains unclear.

### 4.4 Projects

- Any employee can create a new project.
- Project names must be unique.

### 4.5 Flex-Time Account

- The flex-time balance is updated only after the supervisor approves the employee's timesheet.

### 4.6 Working More Than 10 Hours

- Employees must not work more than 10 hours per day.
- If the limit is exceeded, the supervisor should be notified.
- The supervisor prefers a dashboard and email notifications.
- The customer does not want to pay for a dashboard.

**Open point:** Clarify whether email notifications alone are sufficient.

### 4.7 Supervisor Access

- The customer stated that supervisors can access employee data once a month.

**Open point:** Clarify whether this means monthly access to timesheets, monthly approval, or a restriction on how frequently supervisors can view employee data.

### 4.8 Cloud Storage

- The developers may choose the cloud storage provider.
