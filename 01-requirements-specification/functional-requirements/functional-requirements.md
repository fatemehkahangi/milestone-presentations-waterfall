# Functional requirements

- The system shall allow an employee to record begin time, end time and break time for each working day.
- The system shall require a 30-minute break for more than 6 hours of work and a 1-30 hour break for more than 9 hours.
- The system shall lock the time entries of a month after the employee submits the time sheet.
- The system shall update the flex time account only after the supervisor approves the monthly time sheet.
- The system shall notify the supervisor if an employee works more than 10 hours on a day.
- The system shall allow HR to register sickness and shall notify the supervisor of the employee.
