# STC Time Management System - User Handbook

## Scenario: Submitting and Approving a Monthly Timesheet

### Step 1: Employee Submission
1. Log into the system using your employee credentials (e.g., `alex@stc.de` / `test123`).
2. Navigate to the **Timesheet** view from the side menu.
3. Use the date selector to choose the desired month.
4. Add daily time entries specifying begin time, end time, break duration, project, and comments.
5. Once all entries are verified and compliant with daily limits (max 10h) and break rules, click the **"Submit time sheet"** button. The sheet will be locked against further edits.

### Step 2: Supervisor Approval
1. Log into the system as a supervisor (e.g., `ingrid@stc.de` / `test123`).
2. Navigate to the **Approvals** view.
3. Review the list of pending submitted timesheets from your team members.
4. Click **"Review"** to inspect individual time entries.
5. Click **"Approve"** to accept the timesheet (which updates the employee's flex-time balance) or **"Reject"** with a mandatory reason if corrections are needed.
